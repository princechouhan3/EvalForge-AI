## Interpretation

The evaluation dataset compares multiple AI-generated responses against a consistent quality rubric. Responses are assessed for accuracy, relevance, helpfulness, safety, hallucination, clarity, and overall quality.

Preference pairs identify which response provides better overall quality and provide a reason for the selection. This creates structured preference data that can be used for LLM evaluation and preference learning workflows.
